About Me

Hi, I’m Tayiwa Bwala.

I am passionate about creating meaningful impact through my work as a lawyer, researcher, content creator and advocate for global change.

01

Purpose & Service

My work is driven by a commitment to finding innovative solutions that improve lives and strengthen communities. Beyond my professional pursuits, I am actively involved in community-building activities.

As the National Special Duties Coordinator of the Youth Alive Fellowship (YAF) in Living Faith Church Worldwide, I lead initiatives to inspire and empower the next generation of leaders. My faith and dedication to service guides my efforts to create positive change both locally and globally.

02

Tayiwa’s Corner

I am the creator of Tayiwa’s Corner, a content platform where I share faith-based content, reflections, and insights on how serving God and living a life of purpose can lead to fulfillment and growth.

Through platforms like YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, I aim to inspire others, engage in conversations of hope, and build a community rooted in faith, positivity, and support.
